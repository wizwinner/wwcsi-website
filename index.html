OUR COMPANY

WIZ WINNER CONSTRUCTION SERVICES, INC. was established in March 2004, conceived in response to the call for modern implementation of today's technology. WWCSI is a construction company dealing with engineering design, installation, pre-commissioning, start-up operation and maintenance.

The firm was organized to satisfy the need for quality services based on efficient and optimum utilization of resources. We apply skilled and dedicated individuals engaged in residential, commercial, industrial, power plants, petrochemical, refinery, oil & gas plant projects — both here and abroad.
